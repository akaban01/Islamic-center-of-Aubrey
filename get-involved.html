Membership

Become a member of ICA

By becoming a member you affirm your support for ICA’s mission to establish a masjid rooted in the Qur’an, the Sunnah, community service, and Islamic values. Membership grants participation in the governance of ICA through elections and general body meetings, consistent with ICA bylaws.

Eligibility

To be a Regular Member, an applicant must:

  • Be a Muslim who affirms the Shahāda
  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Agree to abide by ICA’s mission, policies, and Islamic code of conduct
  • Reside within an ICA-approved ZIP code

Approved ZIP codes: 76227, 76259, 76266, 75068, 76262, 75033, 75034, 75035, 75036, 75078, 75072, 75071, 75009, 76258

Dues & standing

Annual dues
$50 per member
Collected
Once per year, non-refundable
Hardship waiver
Case-by-case review
Voting
One vote per member; no proxy voting

A member is in good standing when their information is current, dues are paid (or a waiver is approved), they still reside in an eligible ZIP code, and they comply with ICA’s code of conduct. Failure to renew may result in temporary suspension until renewal is completed.

Code of conduct

  • Uphold Islamic manners and adab in all interactions
  • Maintain respect for the Imam, trustees, volunteers, and other members
  • Avoid conduct that causes division, slander, or disruption of community harmony
  • Follow facility rules and committee guidance during events or programs
  • Do not solicit funds on ICA premises or through ICA channels for personal or outside causes without prior approval from the Board or Imam

Your privacy

ICA keeps membership information confidential and will not sell, distribute, or disclose personal data except where required for legal or compliance purposes.

Membership may be suspended or revoked by the Board of Trustees for conduct that harms ICA’s mission, reputation, unity, or operations; for harassment or slander; or for false information given at registration. A reinstatement request may be submitted after a waiting period, in accordance with ICA bylaws.

Membership is a trust (amānah) and a responsibility. Applying for a hardship waiver? Contact the Social Services Committee at socialservices@aubreymasjid.org. Full terms are published on the ICA membership page.
