Sunday School

The ICA Sunday School’s mission is to promote a broader awareness of Islam for Muslim students, enable them to demonstrate exemplary character, and teach them to be effective members of both the Muslim community and the broader American society.

The curriculum is balanced and culturally appropriate — Qur’an reading, surah memorization, Tafseer, Akhlaq, Islamic Studies, and more — taught by experienced, board-certified educators supported by TAs and administrators.

Fiqh

Fiqh is understanding and applying Islamic rulings in daily life. This course offers a structured introduction to the principles and practical topics of Fiqh.

  • Purification
  • Prayer
  • Fasting
  • Zakah
  • Hajj
  • Halal & haram
  • Marriage & family law
  • Contracts & financial transactions

Who should attend: teens and adults looking to deepen their understanding of Islamic practice, new Muslims learning foundational rulings, and anyone interested in how Islamic law applies in real life.

Questions

Before you enrol

What will my child gain from a Qur’an recitation class?

Proper pronunciation and articulation of Arabic letters and words, the rules of Tajweed, and a stronger spiritual connection with the Qur’an. Consistent recitation also builds memorization skills, and classes teach reflective reading — pondering the meanings and applying them day to day. Students benefit from the community of fellow learners alongside the personal growth that comes from patience and discipline.

How do I sign up?

For Maktab, email maktab@aubreymasjid.org for instructions first, then register through the ICA members portal. For Sunday School, register directly through the registration form.

Can I sponsor a student?

Yes. You may sponsor a student and receive the reward for every letter that student learns and recites. Contact admin@aubreymasjid.org to arrange sponsorship.

Are boys and girls taught separately?

In Maktab, yes — classes are separated, with a female instructor for girls and a male instructor for boys.

Who teaches Sunday School?

There are multiple instructors, including experienced board-certified educators, plus teaching assistants and administrators who support the teachers and keep the learning environment safe and beneficial.
