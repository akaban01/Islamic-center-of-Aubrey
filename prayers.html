    How to read these times

    Adhan times, calculated daily

    The times shown are adhan (beginning) times calculated for the masjid’s exact coordinates in Aubrey using the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA) method, in US Central Time. They refresh automatically each day.

    They are not iqamah times. Iqamah — the moment congregational prayer actually starts — is set and announced at the masjid, and it shifts through the year. If you are travelling to pray in congregation, call ahead.

    Jumu’ah

    Friday prayers

    Friday Jumu’ah prayers are offered at two different timings at the Islamic Center of Aubrey to accommodate the community.

    First khutbah

    Khutbah 1:35 PM · Salah 1:50 PM

    Please be sure to leave promptly after the first khutbah to avoid any parking issues for those arriving next.

    Second khutbah

    Khutbah 2:30 PM · Salah 2:45 PM

    If the first prayer is full or you are coming from work, the second khutbah gives you a later option.

    Parking

    Jumu’ah parking guidance

    To keep Jumu’ah respectful and smooth for everyone — including our neighbours — please follow these instructions.

    Where you can park

    • Designated areas highlighted in green on the parking map
    • Greenview Ln, Chattahoochee St, and Spanish Moss Dr
    • Any non-reserved parking spots in the complex
    • Neighbouring streets in the adjacent subdivision, unless otherwise marked

    Where you cannot park

    • In front of business units, homes, mailboxes, or driveways
    • In fire lanes, in front of dumpsters, or blocking other vehicles
    • Along Gardenia Blvd

    Volunteers on site

    Volunteers and security staff are available to guide you to an appropriate spot. Please follow their directions upon arrival.

    Beyond the daily five

    Other prayers at ICA

    Eid prayers

    Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha salah are held each year; timings and venue are announced ahead of the day.

    Janazah

    Funeral prayers and support for bereaved families. Call the masjid as soon as you can so arrangements can be made.

    Taraweeh

    Nightly Taraweeh throughout Ramadan, alongside the regular Isha congregation.

    Eclipse (Kusuf)

    Salat al-Kusuf is observed when a solar or lunar eclipse falls over the area; announcements go out beforehand.

    Need a janazah arranged, or want to confirm an Eid timing? Call (469) 200-2131 or email contact@aubreymasjid.org.
