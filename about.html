Our story

From living rooms to a masjid on Highway 380

The Islamic Center of Aubrey is a non-profit 501(c)(3) religious organization founded in August 2018 by a group of Muslim families in Aubrey, Texas. It serves as a spiritual and community hub — a place for worship, learning, and connection.

In the beginning, the group met in each other’s homes for Friday prayers and other religious activities. As the Muslim community in Aubrey and the surrounding areas grew, the need for a permanent mosque became clear.

In January 2019, ICA rented an office off Highway 380, where the masjid is located today. The founding members and volunteers worked tirelessly to expand, and in 2021 the center moved into a larger space.

Since then, ICA has become an essential part of the local Muslim community. The masjid has undergone several renovations and expansions to accommodate a growing number of worshippers, and it continues to serve as a place of worship, education, and community service.

  • August 2018

    The center is founded

    A small group of Muslim families who had moved to the area establish ICA and begin meeting in each other’s homes for Jumu’ah and religious activities.

  • January 2019

    A permanent home

    ICA rents an office off Highway 380 — the site where the masjid stands today.

  • 2021

    Room to grow

    After sustained volunteer effort, the community moves into a larger space to serve a growing congregation.

  • Today

    A thriving hub

    Daily prayers, two Friday khutbahs, Taraweeh and Eid salah, weekly classes, social services, family nights, interfaith events, and outreach.
