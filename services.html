Social services

Support for anyone who needs it

At the Islamic Center of Aubrey, our dedicated team of volunteers is committed to providing services to our local community. We strive to support individuals and families in need, ensuring their well-being and fostering a sense of unity.

Support reaches beyond our congregation — food distribution, financial assistance, and resource referrals are offered regardless of religion. Community drives have included food for families in need, toys for the Children’s Medical Center, and clothing collections.

Nikah

Marriage at the masjid

The Islamic Center of Aubrey offers nikah services for Muslims to marry at ICA. A nikah is the Islamic marriage contract — a solemn and sacred social and legal agreement between a man and a woman to live together as husband and wife, with both religious and legal significance in Islam.

Mutual consent

Both the bride and groom must freely and willingly consent to the marriage. Forced marriages are not valid in Islam.

Mahr (dowry)

The groom gives a mahr, an obligatory gift, to the bride. It can be money, property, or anything agreed upon, and becomes her exclusive property.

Witnesses

The contract must be witnessed by at least two adult Muslim witnesses, ensuring the marriage is public and official.

Ijab & qubool

The formal offer and acceptance during the ceremony, usually conducted in the presence of an imam or Islamic scholar.

Khutbah

A short sermon is often delivered, highlighting the significance of marriage in Islam and the rights and responsibilities of both spouses.

Marriage counseling

Marriage counseling helps couples work through their differences and improve their relationship. Common issues include communication problems, financial stress, and conflicts over parenting or household responsibilities.

If you and your spouse are interested, reach out to Imam Sajid Ali to schedule an appointment.
